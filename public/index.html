Try the flow

  1. Scanner: tap Upload or Scan to read a photo of a real receipt, or tap Try sample receipt.
  2. Ready to Add: AI sorts the groceries and estimates when each expires. Tap one to fix its name, amount or category, then Add to My Fridge.
  3. My Fridge: filter your inventory, tap an item for expiry and nutrition, or open AR View to place the 3D fridge. Drag to rotate, tap the doors to open them.
  4. Shopping: tick items off, see deals and coupons, and get recipe ideas from what you already have.
  5. Shared kitchen: tap “Shared Kitchen” on My Fridge to switch roommates.

Firebase hookup

When this page is deployed to fridgiprototype.web.app, it switches to live mode on its own. It uses the same sign-in, the same Gemini receipt reader, and the same Firestore data as the team's scanner:

users/{uid}/fridgeItems/{id}
{ name, category, quantity, price,
  addedAt, expiresAt, addedBy }
