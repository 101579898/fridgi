Try the flow

  1. Scan: tap the centre button and use the sample receipt, or upload a photo of a real one.
  2. AI sorts it: items get a category, a shelf and an expiry estimate. Duplicates you already own are flagged.
  3. Store in Fridge: the items drop into the 3D fridge in AR view. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, tap the doors to open them.
  4. Tap any item for its quantity, expiry, nutrition and who added it.
  5. Switch roommate on Home and tap "Used it". Kiara finishing the milk now shows up for everyone.

Firebase hookup

When this page is deployed to fridgiprototype.web.app, it switches to live mode on its own. It uses the same sign-in, the same Gemini receipt reader, and the same Firestore data as the team's scanner:

users/{uid}/fridgeItems/{id}
{ name, category, quantity, price,
  addedAt, expiresAt, addedBy }
